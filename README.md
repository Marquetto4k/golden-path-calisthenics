"Crie uma landing page responsiva para o produto 'Protocolo Calistenia Asiática – Desafio 28 Dias', voltado para homens 40+. Use React, Vite e Tailwind CSS. Design premium nas cores preto, dourado e vermelho, com estilo oriental e foco em conversão. Estrutura inicial: headline, espaço para VSL, botão de compra, benefícios, oferta de R$37 e rodapé. Execution details:
- Responsive high-converting sales landing page for "Protocolo Calistenia Asiática – Desafio 28 Dias" targeting men aged 40+.
- Aesthetic: Premium dark theme with deep blacks (#0B0B0C / #121214), metallic and bright gold accents (#D4AF37 / #E5C158), and balanced crimson/oriental red touches (#B91C1C / #991B1B). Sophisticated martial and disciplined aesthetic.
- Key sections:
  1. Top urgency banner.
  2. Hero section with powerful headline, subtitle tailored to 40+ joint protection & natural strength, and a responsive VSL video placeholder frame with custom overlay and play button.
  3. Prominent CTA buttons with gold glow effect.
  4. Benefits section highlighting joint-friendly calisthenics, 15-20 min daily routines, muscle activation, and vitality after 40.
  5. What is included in the 28-day challenge protocol.
  6. Offer/pricing box featuring R$ 37 (one-time payment highlight), guarantee badge (7-day risk-free), and bonus items.
  7. FAQ section tackling common objections for 40+ beginners.
  8. Footer with disclaimer and terms placeholders.
- Modular component architecture (Hero, VSLPlayer, Benefits, ChallengePhases, OfferSection, FAQ, Footer) for simple copy and asset customization.
